How it works

A tactic in three steps

Everything happens on one screen — position, draw and play without switching modes.

1 — Position

Each dot shows its position. Drag them to build your shape, or drop a formation on in one tap.

2 — Draw runs

Tap a player to select them, then tap or drag the pitch to set where they run. Add arrows anywhere to mark the idea.

3 — Play & export

Press play to preview the whole move, then export it as a looping GIF or an MP4.

The board

A real tactics board, not a slideshow

  • Eleven formations — from 4-4-2 to 3-2-4-1, applied to either side.
  • Both teams on one pitch — with your own names, kits and trim colours.
  • Passing lanes — see which options are actually open from where the ball is.
  • Keyframe timing — perform a move by hand and the app keeps your timing, not just the shape.
  • Annotation arrows — straight or curved, drawn with one gesture.
  • A scene library — with autosave, undo, and duplicate to branch an idea.
  • Six languages — English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish.
  • iPad and iPhone — iOS 17 or later. iPad is where it's happiest.
