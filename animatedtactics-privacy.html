Animated Tactics — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Summary

Animated Tactics does not sell your personal information, and collects none unless you create an account for match data. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you create stays on your device unless you start one of the optional network features below, each of which sends only what its section says. Match data goes through our own service and needs a free account, created with Sign in with Apple; that account and the match-data requests made with it are the only things we receive.

What the app stores

Tactics scenes you save, your projects, your team names, kit colours, and player names — including names, numbers and positions filled in from a real match by the features below — are stored locally on your device. Replies from those features (a day's fixtures, a match's line-ups, a resolved team setup, the position data of a match's moments) are kept in a cache on this device so the same question is never asked twice; you can clear it in Settings. A count of how many exports you have made this month is stored on your device and in your private iCloud key-value storage so your free-tier allowance follows your Apple Account across reinstalls. None of this is ever sent to us, and we have no access to it.

Network use

The app makes network requests only for the optional features below, and only when you start them.

Match data. Signed in to your Animated Tactics account (below), choosing a day sends that date, tapping a match sends that match's identifier, and loading a club's squad sends that club's name and identifier, to our own service at api.animatedtactics.com, which asks API-Football on our account and returns its answer. Our service keeps those answers in a shared cache so the same question is not asked twice, and counts requests per account so that no one account can use up the allowance everyone shares. Its access log records each request's time, network address and the address asked for, and is deleted after seven days. What comes back — the day's fixtures, the two published line-ups, or that club's current squad — fills your rosters and is cached on this device. Nothing about your board is sent.

If you use none of them, the app makes no network requests of its own.

Your account

Match data needs an Animated Tactics account, which you create with Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an identifier for you and, if you allow it, your email address — or a private relay address, if you choose to hide yours. They are kept by Supabase, the sign-in service our server uses, so that you stay signed in and so that requests can be counted per account. The account holds nothing about your boards, scenes or exports, and your session is kept in this device's keychain. You can sign out, or delete the account and everything kept with it, in Settings under Animated Tactics account; deleting removes it from our service at once. Everything else in the app works without an account.

Photos

When you save an exported GIF or video, the app asks for permission to add that file to your photo library. It requests add-only access and cannot read, browse, or modify your existing photos.

Your own logo (Animated Tactics Pro) is picked through Apple's own photo picker, which runs outside the app and hands over only the image you pick — the app is never granted access to your library and cannot see anything else in it. The image you choose is copied into the app's private storage on this device, is used only to draw the board and your exports, and is sent nowhere. Removing it in Settings deletes the copy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple which subscription is active, in order to unlock the features that go with it. Any charge from an AI provider is separate: it is billed by them, to the account whose key you added.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, unless an account is created for match data as described above.
