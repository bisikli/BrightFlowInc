Animated Tactics — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Summary

Animated Tactics does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you create stays on your device.

What the app stores

Tactics scenes you save, your team names, kit colours, and player names are stored locally on your device. A count of how many GIFs you have exported is stored on your device and in your private iCloud key-value storage so your free-tier allowance follows your Apple Account across reinstalls. This data is never sent to us — we operate no servers and have no access to it.

Network use

The app makes no network requests. Nothing you create or configure ever leaves your device.

Photos

When you save an exported GIF, the app asks for permission to add that file to your photo library. It requests add-only access and cannot read, browse, or modify your existing photos.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether an active subscription exists in order to unlock Pro features.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new release of the app. For any question about privacy, contact bilgehan.isikli@gmail.com.
