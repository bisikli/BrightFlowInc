MotionBrush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Summary

MotionBrush does not sell your personal information, show advertising, or track you across apps or websites. Your videos and drawings stay on your device. The only data that ever leaves it is an anonymous crash report, and only if the app crashes.

What the app stores

Projects you create — the imported video reference, your drawings, and their timing — are stored locally on your device. A count of how many videos you have exported is stored on your device and in your private iCloud key-value storage, so your free-tier allowance follows your Apple Account across reinstalls. We operate no servers and have no access to any of it.

Photos

The app asks for photo library access so you can pick a video to draw on, and so it can save your finished video back to your library. Videos you select are read only to compose your export. The app does not upload, scan, or share anything from your library.

Crash reports and diagnostics

If the app crashes or hangs, iOS may hand it a diagnostic report. Those reports are stored inside the app's own container on your device. The app also uses Sentry, a crash-reporting service, to send us the crash itself — the stack trace, the app version, the iOS version, and the device model. These reports carry no name, email, contact details, or advertising identifier, and they never include your videos or drawings. They are used only to find and fix crashes.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether an active subscription exists in order to unlock Pro features.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new release of the app. For any question about privacy, contact bilgehan.isikli@gmail.com.
